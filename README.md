# 🌱 Carbon Coin – Net Zero Campus App

**Carbon Coin** is a gamified mobile app designed to help universities achieve **Net Zero Campuses** by motivating students to adopt sustainable habits. Students earn **Carbon Coins** through eco-friendly actions like recycling and walking, which can be redeemed for real rewards and learning opportunities.

---

## 🔑 Problem

Campuses generate high carbon emissions due to:

* Inefficient waste management
* Excessive vehicle use
* Lack of student motivation for sustainability

---

## 💡 Solution

Carbon Coin makes sustainability engaging by:

* **QR-enabled Smart Dustbins** – Earn coins when disposing waste responsibly
* **Fitness Tracker Integration** – Rewards daily steps and active mobility
* **Safety Checks** – Prevents fake entries during recycling
* **Reward Store** – Redeem coins for:

  * Canteen passes
  * Library cards
  * Event/workshop access
  * Sponsored courses (AI, Cybersecurity, Blockchain, EVs, etc.)
* **Leaderboard** – Encourages healthy competition among students

---

## 🚀 Features

* Secure login (Name, Email, Phone)
* About Us section on Net Zero initiative
* QR Code Scanner for waste tracking
* Integrated step counter for fitness rewards
* Coin wallet + redemption system
* Leaderboards for top contributors

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React Native / Flutter
* **Backend:** Node.js / Firebase
* **Database:** MongoDB / PostgreSQL
* **Integrations:** QR scanner, Google Fit / HealthKit

---

## 🎯 Impact

* Reduces campus carbon emissions
* Promotes sustainable lifestyle habits
* Encourages fitness and wellbeing
* Provides real academic and campus benefits
